    About the reunion sneak peek

    Basketball Wives follows the wives, ex-wives and girlfriends of professional basketball players through business moves, friendships and the fallout that comes with both. The season 11 run split into two halves — 11A opened on January 8, 2024, and 11B premiered on July 1, 2024 before closing with the reunion special, The Reunion: Hot Mics and Spicy Drama, on September 23, 2024.

    A reunion sneak peek is the short first look VH1 releases ahead of that special: the season's unresolved beefs, rebuilt friendships and unfinished business, cut down to a teaser. This page is built around that clip.
