      What it is

      Aired about five weeks before the Season 3 premiere, this special is the franchise's first look at the new-look New York cast. The third season rebuilt the show around Yandy Smith and Rich Dollaz, promoted Erica Mena to the main cast and brought in Winter Ramos, with Joe Budden, Consequence, Tahiry Jose, Raqi Thunda, Jen the Pen, Rashidah Ali and Olivia Longott filling out the ensemble. The special mixes sneak-peek footage with a behind-the-scenes visit to the Love & Hip Hop: Atlanta taping.
