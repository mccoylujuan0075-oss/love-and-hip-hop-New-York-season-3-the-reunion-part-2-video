## Focus episode: S3:E14 — "Reunion – Part 2"

- **Air date:** Monday, April 15, 2013, 8/7c on VH1
- **Runtime:** ~42 minutes · **Rating:** TV-14 · **Viewers:** 2.35 million (season high since the premiere)
- **Host:** Mona Scott-Young · **DJ:** Funkmaster Flex
- **Logline:** The drama unfolds as the ladies of *Love & Hip Hop NY* reunite to get into all the
  issues, the beef, and the madness that occurred during and after the cameras rolled.

### Reunion rundown (what Part 2 covers)

Part 2 picks up where Part 1 (April 8) left off and works through the season's unfinished business:

1. **Cold open / recap** — where every storyline left off after "One Day at a Time."
2. **The roundtable** — the season 3 cast (Yandy, Erica Mena, Jen the Pen, Raqi Thunda, Winter
   Ramos, Rashidah Ali, Tahiry) face off on the gold-couch set against the purple tufted wall.
3. **The beefs** — Erica vs. Raqi, the Tahiry / Joe Budden fallout, and Winter's collisions with
   the cast are re-litigated face to face.
4. **Surprise returns** — Olivia Longott and Lore'l join the conversation; Mendeecees Harris
   appears in archive footage.
5. **The polygraph segment** — certified polygraph examiner Lisa Ribacoff puts the cast's claims
   to the test.
6. **Final words** — closing monologues and the season send-off ahead of the next cycle.
