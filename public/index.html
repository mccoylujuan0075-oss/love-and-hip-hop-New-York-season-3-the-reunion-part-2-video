    Synopsis

    Part two of the season three reunion picks up where part one left off. Host Mona Scott-Young keeps the cast of Love & Hip Hop: New York on the hot seat as the season’s unresolved beefs, love triangles and off-camera fallout get sorted out in front of a studio audience. Filmed in front of the signature purple diamond wall, the special pairs the ladies of the cast with the men at the center of the season’s drama for one last round of receipts, reveals and walk-offs before the season is officially closed out.
