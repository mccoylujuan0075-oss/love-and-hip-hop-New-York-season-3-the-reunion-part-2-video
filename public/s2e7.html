About this sneak peek

Season 2 of Love & Hip Hop: New York ran on VH1 through 2012, and by Episode 7 the season's two biggest storylines were no longer avoidable. The sneak peek is the network's own pre-air tease: the cold open, the confrontation, and the "next on" tag.

This page exists to play that peek properly — adaptive bitrate HLS, chapter list, real photography, and VH1's 2012 gold-on-black on-air look. Nothing is simulated.
