## Where this episode sits

Season 3 ran from **January 7, 2013 to April 15, 2013** — 14 episodes, closing with a two-part reunion hosted by
executive producer Mona Scott-Young.

| Part | Episode | Airdate |

| The Reunion (Part I) | S3 E13 | April 9, 2013 |
| **Reunion: Part 2** | **S3 E14** | **April 15, 2013** |

Part 2 picks up exactly where Part 1 stopped. The set is the show's signature reunion stage: a **purple diamond
lattice wall** backlit in magenta, gold baroque sofas arranged in a shallow arc, and a studio audience just outside
the light.

## Topic threads carried into Part 2

These are the season-long storylines the reunion format works through — the same threads referenced in the episode's
cast list and press materials.

- **Erica & Rich** — the relationship that escalated all season, and what happened once the cameras went down.
- **Joe, Tahiry & Kaylin** — the love triangle that survived the trip to the Dominican Republic and then followed
  everyone back to New York.
- **Yandy & Mendeecees** — the big family decision of the season, revisited with a ring on the table.
- **Raqi & the fallout** — the self-described "troublemaker" who spent the season in the middle of it.
- **New business** — Consequence, Joe Budden and the music the cast was actually in New York to make.
- **Unseen footage** — clips held back from the season, screened for the first time on the reunion stage.
